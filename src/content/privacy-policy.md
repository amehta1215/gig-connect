# SET HOUND PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date: November 17, 2026**

Set Hound ("Set Hound," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at [sethound.com](https://sethound.com) (the "Platform" or "Service"), which connects independent musicians, artists, and venues for the purpose of facilitating communication, performance opportunities, and bookings.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when individuals access or use our Platform.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact **privacy@sethound.com**.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information in connection with operating and improving our Platform:

**A. Account Information**

- Name, email address, and account credentials.
- Artist or venue account type.
- Profile information, including photographs, biographies, venue descriptions, and other information voluntarily provided.
- Information associated with account registration, authentication, and verification.

**B. Communications and Booking Information**

- Messages exchanged between artists and venues.
- Uploaded message attachments and related documents.
- Booking requests, holds, confirmed performances, and associated details.
- Calendar events, performance dates, times, locations, and relevant booking notes.
- Communications with Set Hound regarding customer support or other inquiries.

**C. Technical and Usage Information**

We and our service providers may collect certain technical information, including IP addresses, browser types, device information, and information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the Platform.

Depending on the technologies used, we may also collect information through cookies or similar technologies. Additional disclosures or choices will be provided where required by applicable law.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the Platform to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
- Facilitate communications between artists and venues.
- Process and maintain booking requests, holds, confirmed performances, and calendar records.
- Display artist and venue profiles.
- Provide technical support and respond to inquiries.
- Maintain, improve, and secure the Platform.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorized activity, or violations of our Terms of Service.
- Comply with applicable laws, legal processes, and regulatory obligations.
- Protect the rights, property, and safety of Set Hound, its users, and others.
- Send service-related communications and, where permitted by law, promotional communications.

We may introduce additional features or functionality in the future. We will update this Privacy Policy and provide any additional notices or obtain consent where required by applicable law.

## 3. Profile Visibility and Information Sharing

**Venue Profiles**

Venue profiles may be publicly accessible once published by the venue. Information displayed on public venue profiles may be viewed by individuals who do not have Set Hound accounts.

**Artist Profiles**

Artist profiles are generally accessible only to authenticated users of the Platform.

**Information Shared Between Users**

When artists and venues communicate or engage in booking activities, certain information may be shared between them as necessary to facilitate those interactions.

Users should exercise discretion when sharing personal, financial, contractual, or otherwise sensitive information.

Set Hound cannot control how other users independently retain or use information they have lawfully received through the Platform.

## 4. Messages and Communications

Set Hound provides messaging functionality that allows artists and venues to communicate regarding potential or confirmed performances.

Messages and associated attachments may be stored on the Platform.

Authorized Set Hound administrators may access messages and attachments for legitimate purposes, including operating the Service, providing support, investigating misuse, resolving technical issues, or complying with legal obligations.

**Messages Following Account Deletion**

If a user deletes their Set Hound account, messages previously exchanged with other users may remain accessible to the other participants in those conversations.

The deleted user's name may continue to appear in historical conversations, accompanied by a designation such as "Deleted Artist" or "Deleted Venue."

The deleted user will no longer be able to access the account or send or receive new messages through that account.

Message attachments may remain accessible to authorized participants in retained conversations, subject to applicable retention practices and access controls.

Set Hound may delete retained messages and attachments in accordance with its data-retention practices or as otherwise required by law.

## 5. Booking Records and Calendar Information

Set Hound facilitates booking requests, holds, confirmed performances, and calendar management between artists and venues.

Booking information may include performance dates, times, locations, agreed terms, notes, and other relevant details.

**Confirmed Bookings Following Account Deletion**

Deleting a Set Hound account does not automatically cancel a confirmed performance.

If an artist deletes their account, existing holds and confirmed bookings may remain visible on the venue's calendar, including relevant performance details and the artist's name accompanied by a "Deleted Artist" designation.

If a venue deletes its account, relevant holds and confirmed bookings may remain accessible in the artist's calendar or booking history, including the venue's name, location, and a "Deleted Venue" designation.

These records may be retained to preserve booking history, maintain accurate calendars, support existing arrangements, and address legitimate operational or legal needs.

**Account deletion does not constitute cancellation of a confirmed performance.** Artists and venues remain responsible for communicating regarding cancellations and fulfilling their respective obligations under any agreements between them.

Booking requests associated with deleted accounts may be restricted, removed, or marked as inactive. Requests from deleted artists cannot be accepted as new bookings, and open requests to deleted venues may be removed.

## 6. Account Deletion

Users may delete their Set Hound accounts directly through their account settings.

For security purposes, users must complete the Platform's account-deletion verification procedure, which includes password confirmation and verification through a code sent to their registered email address.

Upon successful account deletion, Set Hound removes the user's active authentication credentials, registered email address, account profile, and associated profile information.

Once an account has been deleted, the former user's email address may be used to register a new Set Hound account.

Creating a new account does not restore the deleted account, its profile, or access to its previous records.

**Certain information may remain following account deletion**, including:

- Messages retained for other conversation participants.
- Attachments associated with retained conversations.
- Holds, confirmed bookings, and related calendar records.
- Certain manually created calendar events.
- Information temporarily retained in system backups.
- Information otherwise retained as permitted or required by applicable law.

Information retained for other users may continue to include the deleted user's former name and details necessary to understand historical communications or bookings.

Users may contact privacy@sethound.com regarding residual personal information or additional deletion requests.

## 7. Uploaded Photos and Files

Users may upload photographs and certain files in connection with their profiles, communications, and use of the Platform.

When an account is deleted, Set Hound's account-deletion process is designed to remove the associated profile photographs from its storage systems.

If an initial storage deletion attempt fails, automated processes may retry deletion.

Once successfully removed from storage, the photographs should no longer be accessible through their former storage URLs, although cached copies or copies independently retained by third parties may remain outside Set Hound's control.

Message attachments associated with retained conversations may remain stored and accessible to authorized participants.

Set Hound may retain or remove uploaded files in accordance with its operational needs, retention practices, and applicable legal obligations.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain legitimate business records, support user interactions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our rights.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose and applicable legal requirements.

Certain information may remain in retained messages, booking histories, or calendar records after an account has been deleted.

We use automated processes to remove certain information in accordance with configured retention rules.

Information deleted from active systems may remain temporarily in backups until those backups expire under the applicable backup-retention schedule.

Users may contact privacy@sethound.com with questions regarding retention or requests concerning their personal information.

## 9. Service Providers and Third Parties

Set Hound may use third-party service providers to support the operation of the Platform, including services for:

- Website hosting and infrastructure.
- Database management and storage.
- Authentication and account security.
- Email delivery and notifications.
- Technical maintenance and customer support.

## 12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Set Hound and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for authentication, security, website functionality, and maintaining user sessions.

**Visitor Analytics**

Lovable collects visitor analytics for us. This analytics uses no cookies, stores nothing on the visitor's device, does not keep IP addresses, and does not identify visitors. [Lovable's privacy policy](https://lovable.dev/privacy) explains how it handles this data.

With the site's current settings, analytics records page visits, where visitors came from, their country, browser, operating system and device type, how long they stay, how fast pages load, errors, and clicks on buttons and links. These are recorded for every visitor; the site does not currently have a consent banner connected to this analytics. Page visits, time on page, page speed and errors are recorded without cookies and without identifying anyone.

Detailed analytics data is kept for 90 days, and daily summaries are kept for one year.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional information or choices concerning nonessential tracking technologies.

Users may also be able to manage certain cookies through their browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may affect Platform functionality.
